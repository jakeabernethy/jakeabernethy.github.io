Jacob Abernethy

Research Scientist, Google Atlanta

Started full-time in 2022, my main gig. I’m a member of the ML Theory Team. Since early 2025 I’ve worked closely with Gemini’s Large-Scale Pretraining team, focusing primarily on model sparsity.

Associate Professor, Georgia Tech

Joined the College of Computing in 2017, promoted to Associate with tenure in 2020. While only holding a 20% position since early 2023, I continue to advise PhD students and postdocs and collaborate with members of the GT community.

BlueConduit, Co-founder

With Eric Schwartz, built a software company to address the difficulty of finding risky assets in cities’ water infrastructure. The company grew out of our work identifying lead pipes during Flint MI’s water crisis.
